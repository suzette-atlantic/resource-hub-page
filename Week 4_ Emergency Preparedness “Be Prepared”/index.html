Build Your Readiness

Preparedness Actions You Can Start Today

Build a safer workplace with practical steps your team can follow before, during, and after an emergency.

Map Evacuation Routes

Create clear, posted routes and verify that every employee knows where to go in an emergency.

Verify Emergency Contacts

Keep local emergency contacts and internal coordinators current and easy to access.

Check and Restock Kits

Review first aid and emergency supplies regularly so expired or missing items do not slow response.

Train and Rehearse

Run short, focused training and annual reviews so your plan stays useful as risks evolve.
